Sound familiar?

You've noticed your hair isn't what it was.

Thinning is one of the most common things clients bring to us, and one of the most fixable to look better. The first step is working out what's going on.

If you're losing hair suddenly or in patches, we'll always suggest checking in with your GP as well.

  • Your parting looks wider than it used to
  • You can see more scalp in photos or under bright light
  • Your ponytail feels thinner in your hand
  • There's more hair in your brush or the shower
  • Your hair feels fine, flat or breaks easily
  • Your scalp is itchy, flaky or tight
