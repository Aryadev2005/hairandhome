Questions

Thinning hair: your questions

Is the scalp check really free?

Yes. It's ten minutes with Sacha and there's no obligation to buy anything or book a treatment.

Does Nioxin regrow hair?

Nioxin is a cosmetic haircare system, not a medical treatment, and it doesn't claim to regrow hair. It's designed to make thinning hair look and feel thicker and fuller, and to keep your scalp in good condition. Some products also help reduce hair fall caused by breakage.

How long before I see a difference?

Many people notice their hair feels thicker and fuller within the first few weeks. Give your routine a proper 30 days, every wash, then pop back in and we'll look at how it's going together.

What causes thinning hair?

Lots of things: genetics, hormones, stress, diet, illness, styling and breakage. We're hairdressers, not doctors, so we can't diagnose medical causes. If your hair loss is sudden or patchy, we'll suggest seeing your GP as well.

Can I still colour my hair if it's thinning?

Usually, yes. The right colour can make hair look fuller, and Nioxin Systems 3 and 4 are made for coloured hair. We'll talk it through at your scalp check.

Do you help men with thinning hair?

Yes. The scalp check is the same for everyone and the Nioxin systems work for all hair types.
